About RBR Pathshala

Established in 2016, RBR Pathshala is dedicated to the motto "Play • Learn • Succeed." We believe in keeping our traditions alive while fostering a happier, healthier, and more united society. This Dandiya Mahotsav is our initiative to bring the community together for a grand celebration of culture.
